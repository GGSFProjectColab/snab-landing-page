## 4. Engineering & Delivery Process

1. **Discovery & Workflow Mapping**: In-depth analysis of business processes, user journeys, operational bottlenecks, and feasibility metrics.
2. **Architecture & Strategy**: Technical stack selection, database schema design, security boundaries, and milestone planning.
3. **UI/UX & Prototyping**: Wireframing, interaction design, and design system creation focused on usability and clarity.
4. **Iterative Sprint Development**: Clean, typed, modular code delivery in bi-weekly sprints with automated CI/CD builds.
5. **Testing, QA & AI Evaluation**: Rigorous unit, integration, stress testing, and prompt/eval testing for reliability and safety.
6. **Production Deployment & Rollout**: Automated deployment to production cloud environments with zero-downtime rollouts.
7. **Observability & Continuous Evolution**: Real-time performance monitoring, security patch management, and ongoing feature iterations.
